Frequently asked questions — Home Cleaning Kauniainen

How much does home cleaning cost in Kauniainen?
Cleava's home cleaning in Kauniainen starts from €39/hour. VAT included. After the household tax deduction the effective hourly rate is about €25.35. The final price is confirmed in the quote based on apartment size and scope.
What areas do you serve in Kauniainen?
We serve all areas of Kauniainen: Kasavuori, Sansinpelto, Tuomarila, Asema, Bemböle and surrounding areas. Our base is in Espoo, so the trip to Kauniainen is short.
What's included in home cleaning?
Standard home cleaning covers vacuuming and washing floors in all rooms, dusting surfaces and furniture, thorough cleaning of the bathroom and toilet, cleaning of kitchen countertops and stove, and emptying bins. Oven and fridge cleaning and window washing can be ordered as extras.
Do you offer service in Swedish?
Yes, because Kauniainen is officially bilingual, many residents speak Swedish. Our service is available in both Finnish and Swedish — just let us know your preference when contacting us.
Do I need to be home during the cleaning?
Not necessary. You can leave a key or give a door code, and our cleaner handles everything. All our cleaners are background-checked and we work reliably even when residents are away.
How quickly can you arrange cleaning in Kauniainen?
We'll contact you the same day. Cleava's base is located in Espoo, so cleaners reach Kauniainen quickly — often the same day you contact us. In most cases we can schedule cleaning for the next day.
