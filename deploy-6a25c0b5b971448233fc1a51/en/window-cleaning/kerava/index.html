Window cleaning Kerava — 20-minute train link, dust from two directions

Kerava is a city of about 37,000 whose appeal rests on the excellent train connection to Helsinki: a 20-minute trip to the main station makes it a popular commuting destination. The railway runs through the city and housing is dense on both sides of the track. Many apartments in Kerava centre and Ahjo have a direct view of the railway.

This is useful as a living environment but also brings challenges for window maintenance. Train braking causes fine-grained brake dust and metal particles that collect on windows, sills and balcony surfaces. Regular window cleaner doesn't remove these effectively. Cleava uses specialist products and methods that bring windows clear without surface damage — for those living near the railway we recommend 2-3 thorough washes per year.

OUR TIPS FOR KERAVA RESIDENTS

Our tips for Kerava residents — effects of railway proximity

In immediate railway proximity (under 100 metres from the track) windows get dirty significantly faster than usual. We recommend Kerava residents wash windows 3 times a year: spring thorough wash (April-May), mid-summer compact wash (June-July) and autumn wash (September-October). If the apartment is further from the track or in a different area (e.g. Ylikerava), 2 times a year suffices.

Second tip: during washing you'll notice that metal particles also collect on sills and balcony railings. This is visually minor but can damage surfaces if left long-term. Cleava's standard service also includes thorough cleaning of frames and sills. Regular washing is often the deciding factor for window longevity.

Frequently asked questions — Window Cleaning Kerava

How long does window cleaning take in Kerava?
A typical Kerava two-room apartment (5-10 windows) takes 1-2 hours, a larger three-room (10-15 windows) 2-3 hours. In a house in Savio or Ylikerava 3-5 hours. For railway proximity we recommend more frequent washing.
How often should windows be cleaned if the apartment is near a railway?
We recommend 3 thorough washes per year in immediate railway proximity (under 100m from the track). Spring, midsummer and autumn. Further from the track, 2 times a year is enough.
What's included in window cleaning?
Standard service includes washing windows on both sides (inside and outside), cleaning frames and trims, and wiping sills. For railway-adjacent apartments, specialist products for brake dust and metal particles.
Do you use special products for railway dust?
Yes. Regular window cleaner doesn't remove metal particles and brake dust effectively — we use specialist products for those that don't damage glass. Let us know if the property is in immediate railway proximity.
Do I need to be there?
Not necessary. You can leave a key or give a door code. Morning slots are popular among working people.
Is window cleaning eligible for the household tax deduction?
Yes. Window cleaning is eligible for the household tax deduction — you can deduct 35% of the labour cost on your taxes, up to €1,600 per person per year.
