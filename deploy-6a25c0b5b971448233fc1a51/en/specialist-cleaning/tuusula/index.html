OUR TIPS FOR TUUSULA RESIDENTS

Our tips for Tuusula residents — challenging situations handled in phases

If you or someone you know feels that restoring the home is too big a task, we recommend starting with a free phone conversation. We go through the situation together and give a realistic assessment of how the cleaning could be handled in phases. The most important thing is taking the first step — conversations with us are always confidential and don't commit you to anything. Even if you eventually decide you don't want the service, the conversation can help frame the situation.

Another angle: handling these kinds of situations typically has several phases. First sorting belongings (keep, recycle, discard), then the actual cleaning and restoration, finally specialist surface treatment when needed. Cleava cooperates with waste removal companies so disposing of belongings is part of the service. When needed we also contact social workers or home care staff if the situation requires broader support. All this happens confidentially, respecting the family's or client's privacy.

Frequently asked questions — Specialist Cleaning Tuusula

Do you approach the situation judgmentally?
Never. Our approach is always non-judgmental — the home gets what it needs, without commentary on the reasons. The client doesn't need to explain or justify. We handle the situation pragmatically.
Is the service confidential?
Yes, absolutely. We don't discuss the property's situation or service details with third parties — neighbors, relatives, employers or other parties. Privacy is important to us.
Can the cleaning be done in phases?
Yes — often the best approach in challenging situations. We start with sorting belongings, move to the actual cleaning, and finish with surface treatments if needed. Scheduling is agreed with the client.
Do you collaborate with social services?
Yes, when needed. If the situation requires broader support, we can collaborate with social workers, home care staff or other professionals. This always happens with the client's consent.
Can you also dispose of belongings?
Yes. We work with waste removal companies, so sorting and disposal of belongings is part of the service. We can also arrange pickup of recyclables if needed.
How is specialist cleaning priced?
Pricing is always tailored to the situation — property size, phased service, scope of needed extras (waste removal, social services cooperation) affect price. We give a free quote after discussion and assessment.
