OUR TIPS FOR KAUNIAINEN RESIDENTS

Our tips for Kauniainen residents — seasonal cleaning rhythm for a spacious home

For spacious Kauniainen houses we recommend semi-annual deep cleaning — once in spring (March-April) and once in autumn (October-November). The spring clean prepares the home for summer and opens guest rooms and balconies for the entertaining season. Frequently asked questions — Deep Cleaning Kauniainen

How long does deep cleaning take in Kauniainen?
A typical Kauniainen house (150-200 m² + attic + basement) takes 8-12 hours with a 2-3 person team. Larger (200+ m²) may require two days. Final time depends on how much attic and basement are included.
Are the attic and basement included in deep cleaning?
Yes — in Kauniainen homes these are an important part of the whole. We systematically go through attic and basement spaces: dust accumulation, moisture check, organizing storage. When contacting us, share the size and contents of the spaces.
Do you offer service in Swedish?
Yes. Kauniainen is officially a bilingual municipality, and our service is available in both Finnish and Swedish. We primarily assign bilingual workers to the area.
What does deep cleaning include and how does it differ from basic home cleaning?
Deep cleaning covers all areas left out by basic home cleaning: oven interior, back of fridge, inside cabinets, lime deposits, ceiling trims, skirting boards, ventilation grilles, inside window sills, door frames. In Kauniainen homes also guest rooms, attic and basement.
How often should deep cleaning be done in Kauniainen homes?
We recommend a twice-yearly rhythm — once in spring and once in autumn. A large house benefits more from two yearly deep cleans than a smaller apartment, because the total space is larger and seasonal variation shows more.
Is deep cleaning eligible for the household tax deduction?
Yes. Deep cleaning is household-tax-deduction eligible — you deduct 35% of labour cost, up to €1,600 per person per year. A typical Kauniainen deep clean (8-12 hours at €40/hour) costs €208-312 after the deduction.
