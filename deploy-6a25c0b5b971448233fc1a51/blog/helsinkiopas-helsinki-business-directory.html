Helsinkiopas.fi — A Guide to Helsinki Services and Businesses

Cleava  ·  5 min read
← Back to blog

Helsinki is Finland's largest city, with hundreds of service providers in every category. Cleaning services, hairdressers, dentists, auto repair shops, restaurants — even with so many options, finding the right reliable provider can take surprisingly long. One handy tool is a local business directory. In this article we look at Helsinkiopas.fi and share practical tips for finding the right service provider for your needs.

What is Helsinkiopas.fi?

Helsinkiopas.fi (which translates literally as "Helsinki Guide") is a free directory and business search service built for the Helsinki business community. It lists companies and public services in the Helsinki area along with their contact details — and it is freely usable by anyone. The site is clearly organized by industry, so you do not need to know the exact name of a business to find it. You just need to know what kind of service you are looking for.

What can you find on Helsinkiopas?

The directory covers a surprisingly broad range of everyday services. A few examples:

  • Cleaning services — home, office and specialty cleaning
  • Accounting firms — bookkeeping and financial services for businesses
  • Dentists and pharmacies — health services
  • Hair salons and beauty parlours — wellness
  • Auto repair and tyre hotels — vehicle services
  • Restaurants, cafés and nightclubs — leisure and dining
  • Funeral homes, veterinarians, massage therapists — specialty services
  • Daycare centres, primary schools and upper secondary schools — education

The site also lists Helsinki museums, theatres, shopping centres and other notable places. In practice, Helsinkiopas serves both residents and people coming to the city as a helpful tool for daily logistics and leisure planning.

Why use a local directory?

Google search shows the largest and most-advertised businesses first. A local directory, on the other hand, gives you a broader view of an industry — including smaller, potentially equally good providers who may not appear at the top of search results. Helsinkiopas is one such tool worth keeping in your toolkit, especially when:

  • You are moving to Helsinki and need to find all your basic services from one place
  • You want to compare multiple providers in the same field at once
  • You are looking for a specialty service that Google does not surface clearly
  • You want to support local Helsinki businesses

How to choose a reliable service provider

Whether you found a provider through a directory, Google, or a friend's recommendation, it pays to verify their reliability with a few checks before booking. Here is a short checklist:

Checklist before booking a service

  • ✓ Business ID (Y-tunnus) is listed — on the company's website or in the YTJ public registry
  • ✓ Google reviews — at least 15 reviews with an average above 4.5
  • ✓ Clear pricing — no hidden fees or mandatory add-ons
  • ✓ Insurance in order — especially for services performed in your home
  • ✓ Up-to-date website — a sign that the business is actively operating
  • ✓ Quick response to enquiries — good customer service starts before the booking

Summary

Helsinki is a big city, and finding a reliable service provider can feel like work. Helsinkiopas.fi is one practical and free tool that gathers the capital's businesses by industry into one place.
