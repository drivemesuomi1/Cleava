5 Reasons Professional Cleaning is Worth It — Even for You

Cleava  ·  5 min read
← Back to blog

Busy days, work, kids, hobbies. Where does cleaning even fit? More and more Finns have found the answer: not in their own time, but with a professional. Here are five concrete reasons why professional cleaning is a smart choice — not just for the wealthy, but for anyone who values their time.

1. You Get Hours Back Every Week

Cleaning a medium-sized apartment takes 2–4 hours. Done weekly, that is over 150 hours a year — nearly four full working weeks. What would you do with that time?

A professional cleaner does the same job faster and more thoroughly, because it is their profession. You can spend that time with your children, on hobbies, resting, or simply doing whatever you actually want.

I let go of the guilt when I realized that every hour spent cleaning is an hour taken away from what I actually want to do.

2. A Cleaner Home Means a Healthier Environment

Professional cleaners use the right tools and methods to effectively remove allergens, dust mites and bacteria. This shows up particularly in:

  • Reduced allergy and asthma symptoms
  • Better indoor air quality
  • Less illness, especially in winter
  • A safer environment for children and elderly family members

3. Stress Decreases — Measurably

Psychological research has shown that living in disorder raises cortisol levels — the body's stress hormone. In a tidy home, the brain gets to rest. It is not just a feeling — it is a physiological response to a clean environment.

4. The Tax Deduction Makes It More Affordable Than You Think

In Finland, professional cleaning services qualify for the household tax deduction. You can deduct 40% of the labour cost — up to 2 100 € per person per year.

5. A Professional Sees What You Miss

When you clean yourself, it is easy to become blind to your own home. A professional cleaner notices the details — dirt building up behind cabinets, the first signs of mould in the bathroom, dust in ventilation grilles. Taking care of small things on time prevents bigger problems later.

From Luxury to Necessity

A decade ago, professional cleaning was seen as a luxury. Today it is a practical choice made by a growing number of working Finnish families. The reason is simple: time is the scarcest resource, and using it wisely is sensible — not indulgent.
